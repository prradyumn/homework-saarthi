About the Scheme
PM Vishwakarma is a Central Sector Scheme launched on 17 September 2023 to provide end-to-end support to artisans and craftspeople practising 18 traditional trades — from recognition through the PM Vishwakarma Certificate & ID Card, to skilling with stipend, modern toolkit incentive, collateral-free credit, digital-transactions incentive, and market linkage.
Recognition

PM Vishwakarma Certificate & ID Card

Skilling

Basic & Advanced training with ₹500/day stipend

Toolkit Incentive

₹15,000 e-voucher for modern toolkit

Credit Support

Collateral-free loan up to ₹3 lakh @ 5%

Market Linkage

Branding, e-commerce & GeM onboarding
