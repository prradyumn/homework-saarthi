When to say “I don’t know”

A wrong maths answer that a parent then teaches their child is far worse than a refusal, because the parent asked precisely because they cannot detect the error. So the gate that decides whether to answer at all was calibrated against a 150-question labelled set rather than set by intuition. The curve came out flat — and that turned out to be the finding.

Why the threshold barely matters

The PRD frames this decision as sweeping one threshold and picking a point. In practice the gate’s behaviour is set almost entirely by categorical layers. Tuning the score alone reached 26% coverage; getting the layers right reached 71% at a lower threshold.

Every refusal class needs its own instrument

A single threshold was being forced up to 0.645 to catch leaks it could never see. Split by class, the metadata signal is perfect on what it was built for — 23 of 23 genuinely out-of-syllabus maths questions, caught with no threshold at all.

Two of my own signals were wrong

Corrected

Decoys by class label were conceptually wrong

“Out-of-syllabus” is a property of the topic, not of the book’s class. Class 6–8 maths revisits fractions, angles, large numbers and area — so a Class 7 fractions chapter is not out-of-syllabus for a fractions question, it is the same concept taught later.

Indexing whole higher-class books captured 41 of 100 legitimate parent questions as the top match, forcing the gate to refuse 76% of real questions. Restricting decoys to 19 topic markers verified absent from the Class 5 corpus cut 1,275 decoy chunks to 516 and halved the capture rate. The rejected candidates make the point: गुणनखंड appears 31 times in Class 5, क्षेत्रफल 53, चर 60 — and गुणनखंड is exactly why a Class 9 algebra question scored 0.569 against a Class 5 textbook.

Corrected

The figure flag was mis-calibrated in both directions

As a gate signal, figure_dependent discarded 12 of 100 answerable questions while still missing 3 of 4 figure-only ones. The signal was in the wrong place: a figure-only question is identified by the question pointing at a figure, not by the chunk containing one.

Refining it mattered too. Refusing on any figure reference broke नक्शे में दिशाएँ कैसे देखते हैं? — a method question, answerable from text. A figure reference now refuses only alongside a value-seeking interrogative, and never alongside कैसे, मतलब or क्यों.

The finding that unlocked 71%

One question defeated every signal: शीला ने रमन की अपेक्षा अध्ययन पर कितने घंटे अधिक समय व्यतीत किया? It names no figure, and it retrieves exactly the right chunk. But its answer lives in the heights of a bar chart, which no sentence on the page states.

A numeric-answerability check — value-seeking question, chunk states no numbers — should have caught it, and didn’t. Because the chunk is full of numbers:

… घंटों की संख्या 8 7 6 5 4 1 0 … 10 9 गतिविधियाँ …

Those are the chart’s y-axis tick labels, pulled in by the PDF text layer. The chunk looks numeric while containing no data. 61 of 248 chunks carry runs like it — noise in the text, and enough to defeat the answerability check. Detecting them explicitly took coverage from 26% to 71%.

What this does not yet show

  • This is the gate’s curve, not the product’s. A 1.4% wrong-answer rate means “1.4% of answered questions should have been refused” — not that 1.4% of answers were factually wrong. Answer accuracy needs generation, which is the next milestone.
  • Refusal on legitimate traffic is ~29%, just outside the 25% guardrail. The binding constraint is no longer the threshold: 21 of 100 real questions still retrieve a decoy as their best match.
  • One residual leak at the operating point — तीन भिन्नों को जोड़ना है जिनके हर अलग हैं (adding unlike denominators, 0.639). Genuinely beyond Class 5, but its nearest neighbour is the Class 5 section on same denominators, and no decoy covers it.
  • The 100 in-syllabus questions are authored, not observed. They deliberately use parent vocabulary — एक जैसी भिन्न rather than the textbook’s तुल्य भिन्न — but they remain my guesses about how parents speak. The discovery interviews are what make this set real, and they have not happened yet.
